# Research: AI-assisted codebook development

Gathered 2026-10-06 before specifying, so the design follows published practice instead of guesses.

## Findings

- **Humans keep conceptual authority.** Human-LLM inductive coding pipelines have the model generate candidate labels and annotations while researchers own category definitions and the interpretive frame; hybrid codebooks were applied more reliably and rated higher than fully automated ones ([Human-LLM Collaborative Inductive Coding, arXiv 2607.28889](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.28889); [How K-12 Educators Use AI, arXiv 2507.17985](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.17985)).
- **Proposal shape.** LLM-drafted codebooks are evaluated as name, definition, inclusion/exclusion criteria and examples. Naming the guiding theory produced the most usable codebook; supplying full theory texts improved alignment but reduced usability ([Zambrano et al. 2026, "Data Plus Theory Equals Codebook", JEDM](https://jedm.educationaldatamining.org/index.php/JEDM/article/view/1001)). Qualia's existing code fields already match this shape; a short optional *focus* text plays the "name the theory" role.
- **Commercial tools present suggestions, the researcher decides.** MAXQDA's new-code suggestions accept optional extra instructions, store the explanation in the code memo and require selecting suitable suggestions; they ignore existing codes and include no example quotes ([MAXQDA help](https://www.maxqda.com/help/ai-assist/ai-new-codes-suggestions)). ATLAS.ti "Suggested Codes" works on a selected passage and the user chooses what to apply ([ATLAS.ti help](https://atlastihelp.helpscoutdocs.com/article/732-how-to-use-suggested-codes-in-atlas-ti)). Qualia improves on both by sending existing codes (avoid duplicates) and requiring verbatim evidence.
- **Known failure modes.** Evaluators preferred LLM codes 61% of the time, but models fragmented data, missed latent interpretations and produced themes with unclear boundaries ([Martinez Montes et al. 2025, arXiv 2510.18456](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18456v1)). Fabricated or misattributed quotes are a recurring risk; exact-match quote rates vary widely by model and the recommended control is to verify every quote against the source or drop it ([GoTranscript QA checklist](https://gotranscript.com/en/blog/ai-qual-research-qa-checklist-prevent-hallucinations-misattribution); [Turku hallucination-reduction study](https://research.utu.fi/converis/portal/detail/Publication/508353802?lang=en_GB)).
- **Disagreements are refinement evidence.** Human–LLM coding disagreements and model uncertainty can be mined to find ambiguous definitions and refine the codebook rather than treated only as errors (CHALET, CLARIFY and related work: [arXiv 2405.05758](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.05758); [Xiao et al., codebook + GPT-3 deductive coding](https://ar5iv.org/html/2304.10548)).
- **Methodological limits.** Braun, Clarke and 400+ qualitative researchers reject generative AI for reflexive thematic analysis and other "Big Q" approaches as methodologically incongruent ([SSRN 5676462](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=5676462)). Qualia therefore labels proposals as optional codebook assistance for codebook-based approaches, never theme generation, and keeps every proposal and decision exportable for disclosure.

## Design consequences

1. Proposals only; a person accepts into the draft codebook (optionally editing) or rejects with a note. Nothing reaches a frozen version without the person freezing it.
2. Every example is verified against the supplied segments and stored in the segment's exact wording; an example that cannot be found is removed and the removal is reported. Structural errors (unknown segment or code, duplicate name) reject the whole response, naming the record.
3. Existing codes are sent with draft requests so the model proposes additions, not duplicates; names must not collide.
4. Refinement evidence comes from the researcher's own review decisions and notes (rejected suggestions), unused codes and overlapping pairs — computed offline without AI. Evaluation metrics are not used (they feed the measured improvement policy; keeping them out avoids tuning the codebook to the judge).
5. Copy states the limits: proposals can fragment, miss latent meaning and blur boundaries; they are not themes; disclose AI assistance in methods.
